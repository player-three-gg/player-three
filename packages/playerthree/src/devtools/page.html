      Agents
      
        
      Every owner with a commander roster: one commander per role, the agents signed in for them, and what they did recently. Rotate a commander's token or revoke a sign-in here. Refreshes every 2 s.

      Policies
      
        
      Every owner's policy library per role, the bot pool other players fight, and how policies fail: crashes, timeouts and errors. Disable or unpublish pool entries here. Refreshes every 2 s.
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
    
      

    
    
      
      
      
        
      Bench
      
        
      Read-only canonical series results: entrant standings, ratings, usage coverage, decision latency and recording completeness. Accepted matches and excluded attempts retain their entrant-to-side schedules; available replay artifacts open in the timeline. Series are started from the command line.
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
    
      

    
    
      
      
      
        
      Safety
      
        
      What agents can see and do on this server: the surface hash, enabled modes and their limits, default seat scopes, the game's Safety Card, and which inputs get refused. Refreshes every 2 s.
      
      
      
      
      
    
      

    
    
      
      
      
        
      Game settings
      
        
      The game's match options, the server's defaults and the options each live or recent session runs with. Read-only: rules are the game's to define and your server's to pass. Refreshes every 2 s.
